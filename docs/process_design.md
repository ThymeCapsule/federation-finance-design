# Process design by Workday Financials area

For each area: what the repo models in code, what is design only, the worktag coding rule, the key control, and the main dependency on another area. "In the repo" means it is in the data and tested; "design only" means it is described here and not built.

| Area | In the repo | Design only | Coding rule (worktags) | Key control | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Record-to-Report** | Journals with source, preparer and approver; trial balance; intercompany and elimination; results by campus, sector and fund; budget ledger and report 08 | Close calendar, accruals, reversing journals, consolidation beyond a single elimination | Company, account, cost center and fund on P&L lines; project on grant lines | Checks 1, 6, 10; period-end reconciliations | Every other area, since all post to the ledger |
| **Procure-to-Pay** | AP subledger (28 invoices, payments); invoices coded to cost center and fund; capex invoices linked to assets | Requisitions, purchase orders, receipt matching, supplier onboarding, payment runs | Cost center and fund (and project for research spend) copied from requisition to invoice | Check 12 (AP ties to 2000); SoD pairs on invoice entry, approval, supplier master and payment | Assets (capex), Projects (research cost), Security |
| **Order-to-Cash** | AR subledger for student and commercial billing, receipts; report 09 | Student billing feed, customer master, credit control, refunds, write-offs | Revenue account decides fund and cost center; student revenue to the delivering cost center | Check 12 (AR ties to 1100); SoD between invoicing and cash application | Student system integration, Reporting |

## Cross-area flow worth designing early

A research grant touches five areas: a supplier invoice or expense report captures the cost (P2P, Expenses), the project and fund validate the coding (FDM), grant revenue is recognised against income in advance (Projects), the sponsor is invoiced (Project billing, O2C), and the restricted fund balance is reported (Reporting). A fault in any one shows up in the others, which is why the grant controls are tested across the ledger rather than inside one module. The dependency matrix and risks are in `design_governance.md`.
